# Short Descriptions for Prosperity & Growth

**Option 1: The "Engine of Growth"**
*   **Track Name**: Engine of Prosperity
*   **Short Description**: We flip the script from saving failing businesses to accelerating successful ones. Our agent finds the "economic engines"—businesses whose expansion creates the largest ripple of indirect jobs at their suppliers—and targets them to maximize neighborhood job growth per grant dollar.

**Option 2: The "Targeted Prosperity" Approach**
*   **Track Name**: Targeted Prosperity
*   **Short Description**: To lower unemployment, we use the graph to trace economic impact backward. Our agent identifies applicants whose growth will generate the most downstream hiring, specifically targeting supply chains rooted in high-poverty census tracts to drive equitable economic growth.

**Option 3: The "Job Multiplier"**
*   **Track Name**: The Prosperity Multiplier
*   **Short Description**: Instead of funding businesses on the brink, we fund businesses ready to grow. By analyzing the supply chain, our agent calculates the "employment multiplier" of every applicant, directing grants to the businesses that will generate the highest total job creation across the corridor.
