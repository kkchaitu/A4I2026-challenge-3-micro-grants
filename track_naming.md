# Track Name Options

Here are a few ways to name and frame this track, depending on how you want to pitch it to the judges. 

### Option 1: The "Job Multiplier" Track (Most Direct)
*   **Name**: 📈 The Job Multiplier Track
*   **The Pitch**: "Instead of using grants as life support for failing businesses, we use them as seed capital for job creation. This track flips the graph backward to find businesses with the highest 'employment multiplier'—meaning their growth creates the most indirect jobs at their suppliers. We target grants to lower unemployment by maximizing the total job footprint per dollar spent."
*   **Why it works**: It clearly states the mechanic (multipliers) and the outcome (jobs).

### Option 2: The "Economic Engine" Track (More Ambitious)
*   **Name**: ⚙️ The Economic Engine Track
*   **The Pitch**: "We treat the commercial corridor not as a list of independent shops, but as an engine. When you pour fuel (a grant) into the right gear, it spins the rest of the machine. This track identifies the 'engine' businesses—those whose supply chains are deeply rooted in high-poverty census tracts. By funding them, we drive economic growth and job creation down into the neighborhoods that need it most."
*   **Why it works**: It uses a great metaphor and ties the graph traversal directly to the equity audit.

### Option 3: The "Targeted Prosperity" Track (Focuses on Equity)
*   **Name**: 🌱 Targeted Prosperity
*   **The Pitch**: "A rising tide doesn't lift all boats if the water can't reach them. This track is about targeted prosperity. We use the property graph to trace the flow of money backward from an applicant to their suppliers. By calculating the 'Supply Chain Poverty Footprint,' our agent finds the exact businesses whose growth will disproportionately create jobs for residents in high-need census tracts, actively lowering localized unemployment."
*   **Why it works**: "Prosperity" is a strong, positive word, and "Targeted" shows you are using data (the graph) deliberately.
